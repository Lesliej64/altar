# Print-on-Demand — Launch Kit

Sell T-shirts, mugs, posters, and more without holding inventory. You make designs; the POD company prints and ships each order.

## 1. Choose your setup

| Option | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Redbubble** (also Teepublic, Spreadshirt) | Upload designs to their marketplace; they handle everything | Free, fastest start, built-in traffic | Low margins, little control, crowded |
| **Printful or Printify + Etsy** | You run an Etsy shop; Printful/Printify prints and ships automatically | Etsy has buyers already searching; better margins; your own brand | Etsy fees (listing + transaction + payment processing); you handle customer service |
| **Printful/Printify + Shopify** | Your own store | Full control, best long-term | Monthly fee, and you must bring all the traffic yourself |

**Recommended:** start on **Redbubble** (free, practice designs) **and Etsy + Printify or Printful** (real income). Add Shopify later once you have proven bestsellers.

Check current fees on each platform before pricing — they change.

## 2. Pick niches (the most important step)

Broad designs ("cool dog shirt") don't sell. Specific identities do: people buy things that say *who they are*.

Formula: **[Identity/hobby] + [specific twist]**

| Too broad | Better niche |
|---|---|
| Dogs | Corgi moms, retired greyhound adopters |
| Nurses | ICU night-shift nurses, nursing students |
| Fishing | Bass fishing grandpas, kayak fishing |
| Teachers | Kindergarten teachers, special ed teachers |
| Gardening | Houseplant collectors, beekeepers |

### How to check a niche (free)

1. Type it into Etsy search and watch the autocomplete suggestions — those are real searches.
2. Look at results: listings with many reviews mean demand; a few hundred results or fewer means less competition.
3. Check Google Trends for seasonality.
4. Browse Reddit/Facebook groups for the niche — inside jokes and phrases make the best designs.

## 3. Making designs

- **Tools:** Canva (easiest, has POD-friendly templates), Kittl (made for T-shirt designs), Photopea (free Photoshop-like editor), Inkscape (free vector editor).
- **Text designs sell well and are easy:** a funny or proud phrase in a good font beats complex art.
- **Specs:** use transparent PNGs at high resolution (e.g. 4500×5400 px for shirts; check each platform's template).
- **AI image tools** can help with ideas and artwork, but:
  - Follow each platform's rules on AI-made content (Etsy, for example, asks sellers to disclose AI use in the listing).
  - In the US, purely AI-generated art generally can't be copyrighted, so others may copy it. Adding your own text and editing helps make work more your own.

### Avoid takedowns and legal trouble

Never use: brand names or logos, sports teams, movie/TV/game characters, song lyrics, celebrity names or faces, or trademarked phrases. Before using a catchy phrase, search it on the USPTO trademark search (tmsearch.uspto.gov). Repeated violations can get your shop shut down.

## 4. Listing for search (Etsy)

- **Title:** lead with what buyers type — "Corgi Mom Shirt, Funny Dog Mom Gift, Corgi Lover Tee".
- **Tags:** use all 13, mixing phrases (gift for her, corgi owner gift, dog mom tshirt…).
- **Mockups:** lifestyle mockups (people wearing the shirt) outperform flat images. Printful/Printify and Canva generate them.
- **Pricing:** base cost + Etsy fees + shipping + your profit. Aim for roughly $5–10 profit per shirt to start. Many shops include shipping in the price so they can offer "free shipping".
- **Order a sample** of your first products to check print quality and take your own photos.

## 5. 30-day plan

| Week | Goals |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open Redbubble + Etsy shops; connect Printify or Printful to Etsy. Research and choose 3 niches. |
| 2 | Make 10 designs per niche (30 total). Upload to Redbubble across many products. |
| 3 | List your best 15–20 designs on Etsy with strong titles, tags, and mockups. Order 1–2 samples. |
| 4 | Keep adding 3–5 designs per day. Check stats: which listings get views and favorites? Make more designs like those. |

**Key mindset:** POD is a numbers game. Most designs won't sell; a few will sell repeatedly. Shops often need 50–100+ listings before steady sales, and it can take 2–3 months. Plan for the long run.

## 6. Growth later

- Seasonal designs (Mother's Day, Christmas, back-to-school) — upload 6–8 weeks early.
- Expand winners to more products (hoodies, mugs, tote bags, stickers).
- Pinterest pins linking to listings drive free traffic.
- Etsy Ads in small amounts on proven listings only.
