# AI Automation Consulting — Launch Kit

Set up chatbots, email automations, and workflows (Zapier, Make, Claude/ChatGPT) for small businesses.

## 1. Pick a niche (do this first)

Generalists struggle; "AI automation for **[one industry]**" sells. Pick one where owners are busy, get lots of repetitive messages, and lose money on missed leads:

| Niche | Painful repetitive work you can automate |
|---|---|
| Real estate agents | Lead follow-up, showing scheduling, listing descriptions |
| Salons / spas / barbers | Booking reminders, no-show follow-up, review requests |
| Contractors (roofing, HVAC, plumbing) | Quote-request intake, missed-call text-back, follow-ups |
| Dentists / clinics | Appointment reminders, FAQ chatbot, intake forms |
| Restaurants / cafés | Catering inquiries, review replies, social posts |
| Coaches / consultants | Lead capture, onboarding emails, call summaries |

## 2. Your service packages

Start with fixed-price packages — easier to sell than hourly. Prices below are typical starting ranges; adjust for your market and raise them as you collect results.

| Package | What's included | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Win** | One automation, e.g. web form → CRM/spreadsheet → instant email/text reply | $300–750 one-time |
| **Lead Machine** | Lead capture + AI-drafted follow-ups + review-request sequence | $1,000–2,500 one-time |
| **AI Assistant** | Website/FAQ chatbot trained on the business's info, with handoff to a human | $1,000–3,000 setup |
| **Care Plan** | Monitoring, fixes, small changes, monthly report | $200–750 / month |

The Care Plan is where steady income comes from — offer it with every build.

## 3. Tool stack

| Purpose | Tools | Cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow automation | Zapier, Make (Make is usually cheaper for complex flows) | Free tiers to start |
| AI | Claude API, ChatGPT/OpenAI API | Pay per use, usually cents per task |
| Chatbots | Chatbase, Voiceflow, Tidio, or custom via Make + AI API | Free/low tiers |
| Forms & data | Google Forms/Sheets, Tally, Airtable | Free tiers |
| Email | Gmail, Mailchimp, MailerLite | Free tiers |
| Scheduling | Calendly, Cal.com | Free tiers |

Clients usually pay for their own tool subscriptions; you bill for setup and care.

## 4. Build 3 demos before you pitch (Week 1)

Demos close deals. Build these on free tiers using a fake business in your niche, and record a 2-minute screen video of each (Loom is free):

1. **Instant lead reply** — form submission → AI writes a personalized reply → email sent in under 1 minute → row added to a Google Sheet.
2. **Review requester** — new completed job in a sheet → wait 1 day → friendly email/text asking for a Google review.
3. **FAQ chatbot** — chatbot that answers hours, prices, services, and books a call.

## 5. Finding clients

- **Warm network first:** tell everyone you know what you do; ask "who do you know that runs a small business?"
- **Local outreach:** 10 businesses per day. Look them up on Google Maps; note slow review replies, no online booking, contact forms that go nowhere.
- **Free audit offer:** "I'll find 3 tasks you could automate — free, 20 minutes." It converts much better than a cold pitch.
- **Online:** Upwork / Fiverr ("Zapier", "Make.com", "AI chatbot" gigs), local Facebook business groups, LinkedIn posts showing your demos.

### Cold email template

> **Subject:** quick idea for [Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I noticed [specific thing — e.g. your contact form doesn't send a confirmation / it takes a few days to reply to reviews].
>
> I help [niche] businesses set up simple automations so leads get an instant reply and follow-ups happen automatically — no new software for you to learn.
>
> Here's a 2-minute video of one I built: [Loom link]
>
> Would a free 20-minute call to find 3 things you could automate be useful?
>
> [Your name] · [phone]

### Free audit call — questions to ask

1. Walk me through what happens when a new customer contacts you.
2. What do you or your staff do over and over every week?
3. Where do leads or customers fall through the cracks?
4. What tools do you already use (email, calendar, booking, spreadsheets)?
5. If one task disappeared tomorrow, which would you pick?

End with: "Based on this, I'd start with [one automation]. It's a fixed $[X], done in [N] days. Want me to send a one-page proposal?"

## 6. Doing the work responsibly

- Use a short written agreement: scope, price, timeline, what happens after delivery, and who pays for tools.
- Keep human review on anything the AI sends to customers until the client trusts it.
- Don't put sensitive data (health, payment, ID numbers) through AI tools without the client's consent and a proper setup. Healthcare clients bring privacy rules (e.g. HIPAA in the US), so either get informed or avoid that data.
- Document every build (a simple Google Doc: what it does, logins, how to turn it off).
- Collect a testimonial and a before/after number ("replies went from 2 days to 2 minutes").

| Week | Goals |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pick niche. Learn Zapier/Make basics (free tutorials). Build the 3 demos and record videos. |
| 2 | One-page website or Google Doc portfolio. List 50 target businesses. Start outreach: 10/day. |
| 3 | Run free audits. Offer your first client a discounted "founding client" price for a testimonial. |
| 4 | Deliver first project. Offer Care Plan. Ask for referrals. Raise prices for the next client. |

**First-month target:** 1–2 paying clients and 1 testimonial.
