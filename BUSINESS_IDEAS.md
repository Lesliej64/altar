# Home Business Ideas (Computer-Based)

A shortlist of businesses you can run from home with just a computer, grouped by how you get started.

## 1. Start this week (low cost, skill-based)

| Business | What you sell | Startup cost | Time to first income |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freelance writing / editing | Blog posts, website copy, résumés, proofreading | ~$0 | Days–weeks |
| Virtual assistant | Email/calendar management, data entry, customer support | ~$0 | Days–weeks |
| Bookkeeping | Monthly books for small businesses (QuickBooks/Xero) | $0–300 | Weeks (recurring) |
| Social media management | Running accounts for local businesses | ~$0–50/mo | Weeks |
| Résumé & LinkedIn writing | Rewrites for job seekers | ~$0 | Days |
| Online tutoring | School subjects, test prep, languages, coding | ~$0 | Days (Wyzant, Varsity Tutors) |

## 2. Build an asset (slower start, scales)

| Business | What you sell | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Digital products | Printables, planners, Notion/Canva templates, spreadsheets (Etsy, Gumroad) | Make once, sell many times |
| Print-on-demand | T-shirts, mugs, posters (Printful, Redbubble) | No inventory; success depends on design and niche |
| Online course / newsletter | Teaching something you know well | Needs an audience; pairs with consulting |
| Small software / web apps | Tools, browser extensions, sites | AI coding tools make this far more accessible |

## 3. Technical / specialized services (higher rates)

- **Websites for local businesses** — Squarespace/Wix/WordPress; $500–3,000 per site plus monthly maintenance.
- **AI automation consulting** — chatbots, email automations, Zapier/Make workflows for small businesses.
- **Video editing** — for YouTubers, podcasters, course creators.
- **Graphic design** — logos, brand kits, social graphics.

## Recommended path

Start with a **service** (VA, writing, bookkeeping, or websites). It pays fast, costs almost nothing, and shows you what people will actually pay for before you build a product.

### 30-day launch plan

1. **Week 1 — Pick a niche.** One skill + one customer type (e.g. "social media for local restaurants").
2. **Week 1 — Portfolio.** A one-page site or Google Doc with 2–3 samples (free/sample work is fine).
3. **Weeks 2–3 — Outreach.** Contact 20 local businesses directly and/or list on Upwork or Fiverr.
4. **Week 4 — Formalize.** Once clients repeat: register the business, open a separate bank account, track income for taxes (rules vary by state/country).

### Red flags to avoid

- "Work from home" kits or anything requiring an upfront fee to start
- MLMs / recruitment-based schemes
- Paying for "job leads" or "guaranteed clients"
