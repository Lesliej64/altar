# Altar — Home Business Plans

Running two computer-based home businesses side by side:

| Business | Plan | Pays off |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Automation Consulting** | [ai-automation/README.md](ai-automation/README.md) | Fast — first client can come within weeks |
| **Print-on-Demand** | [print-on-demand/README.md](print-on-demand/README.md) | Slow build — passive sales over months |

Full list of ideas considered: [BUSINESS_IDEAS.md](BUSINESS_IDEAS.md)

## Why these two work together

- **Consulting** brings in money now; **POD** grows quietly in the background.
- Automation skills help POD: e.g. a Make/Zapier flow that logs Etsy sales to a sheet, or AI that drafts listing titles and tags.

## Suggested weekly split (≈15–20 hrs)

| Time | Focus |
|---|---|
| ~70% | AI automation — learning, demos, outreach, client work |
| ~30% | POD — 1–2 sessions a week making designs and listings in batches |

Shift toward consulting whenever client work comes in; POD can always wait a week.

## Month-1 checklist

### AI automation
- [ ] Pick a niche
- [ ] Learn Zapier/Make basics
- [ ] Build and record 3 demos
- [ ] Simple portfolio page
- [ ] Contact 50 businesses
- [ ] Run free audits
- [ ] Land first client + testimonial

### Print-on-demand
- [ ] Open Redbubble + Etsy shops; connect Printify or Printful
- [ ] Choose 3 niches
- [ ] Create 30 designs
- [ ] List 15–20 on Etsy
- [ ] Order samples
- [ ] Review stats and double down on what gets views
